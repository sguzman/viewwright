# M43 — Stop Condition

Stop M43 when the Lantern Leaf reader can author and render a rich, locally identified chapter structure plus one representative spoken range.

Required:

- legacy title+paragraph documents remain valid and visually unchanged;
- rich document form exists;
- eyebrow/heading/paragraph/quote/divider blocks validate and render;
- block IDs are local and unique;
- spoken range validates Unicode-scalar offsets;
- fixture-aware text projections expose block structure and spoken range;
- soft spoken-range highlight uses existing palette derivation only;
- document element remains sole M34 author identity for its fixture content;
- expectation remains 0.2;
- M35 unchanged;
- ViewWitness unchanged;
- new M43 specimen passes exact verification;
- human QA accepts the richer reader and soft highlight;
- no font binding;
- no document presentation-control binding;
- no EPUB/TTS runtime;
- no visual-schema expansion;
- no responsive work;
- no M44.

After acceptance, reassess whether the strongest remaining north-star pressure is richer visual/typographic authorship, transport/progress/media semantics, or responsive variants.
