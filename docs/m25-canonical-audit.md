No accepted canonical `design.character` or `design.avoid` entry is empty or whitespace-only. Omitted/default-empty lists remain legal by M25 authority.

No canonical source migration, concept-output change, renderer change, or visible-output change is expected for M25. Valid authored vocabulary remains byte-for-byte unchanged in resolved design intent and concept projection.

If implementation discovers a contradictory accepted source, stop and report it rather than silently editing canonical source.
