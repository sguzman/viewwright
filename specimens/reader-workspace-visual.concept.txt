ViewWright concept specification

Screen
  id: reader_workspace_visual
  purpose: Read a document in a calm, deliberate workspace

Design character
  quiet
  focused
  crafted
  reading-first
  restrained

Avoid
  toolbar dominance
  uniform visual weight
  nested card soup
  border everything
  control clutter
  pill soup

Hierarchy
  dominant: reader
  supporting: library, app_commands, transport
  tertiary: inspector

Composition
  workspace — vertical
    app_commands — fixed 52px, transparent
    reading_body — horizontal
      library — fixed 250px, panel
      reader — growing, canvas, primary
      inspector — fixed 300px, panel, tertiary
    transport — fixed 68px, raised

Palette
  canvas: #101216
  surface: #171A20
  surface_raised: #1E222A
  text: #E8EAF0
  text_muted: #9299A6
  accent: #8DB7C7
  border: #2B313B

Typography
  display: 26
  heading: 18
  body: 14
  caption: 12

Surface policy
  primary reading surface should feel continuous and calm
  supporting panels should remain visually subordinate
  transport may separate from the canvas without becoming a dominant toolbar
  borders are minimal rather than universal
  corners use the medium token (7)

Important semantic elements
  command: Open
  search: Search library
  tree: Contents
  document: Document
  property sheet: Reading settings
  primary command: Play / pause
  commands: Voice, Speed
  status: Reading status

Concept-art direction
  Desktop reading workspace with a quiet dark technical aesthetic.
  The reading surface is visually dominant and spacious.
  Navigation and inspection remain available but subdued.
  Controls should look deliberate and integrated, not like a generic dashboard.
  Avoid excessive cards, pills, borders, gradients, and decorative chrome.

This file is an illustrative target projection, not canonical source.
